# InterviewScribe brand system

Read `docs/UI_UX.md` and use `assets/interviewscribe-logo.svg` as the approved master logo. Preserve its two opposing speech bubbles and three centered transcript lines. Never promote another proposal to production without explicit user validation.

Preserve the approved SVG geometry, flat `#3156A3` color, clear space and small-size legibility. Do not replace, merge, stretch, rotate, outline or rearrange the bubbles and transcript lines. Do not use gradients, shadows, glass, 3D, microphones, headphones, waves, quotation marks or AI sparkles.

The UI remains neutral, editorial and content-first. Meet WCAG AA, provide visible focus, use 44 by 44 pixel touch targets and support light and dark themes. Derive PNG, ICO and Android icons only from the approved master SVG and verify centering at every size.
