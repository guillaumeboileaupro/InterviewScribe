# UI/UX design

Read `docs/UI_UX.md` and the relevant product flow before editing the interface.

Aim for a restrained, premium editorial tool. Favor hierarchy, typography, spacing and immediate state clarity over decoration.

Required behavior:

- Keep the transcript as the visual focus.
- Make recording, paused, processing, error and saved states unmistakable.
- Use speaker colors only as supporting identifiers.
- Provide keyboard access and visible focus on desktop.
- Use 44 by 44 pixel minimum touch targets on mobile.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast and never rely on color alone.
- Support narrow mobile layouts without shrinking essential controls.
- Avoid gradients, glass effects, excessive rounding, oversized headings and decorative animation.

Before finishing, inspect loading, empty, error, recording and long-transcript states in both light and dark themes.
